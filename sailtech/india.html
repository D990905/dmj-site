The squad

2026 is the year this team went global.

A World Championship start, a European Championship where the squad measured itself against the deepest youth fleet in the sport, and the Asian Championships in Langkawi — three continents of racing in a single season.

Behind the results is a professional program rare at youth level anywhere: every race analysed against the fleet's best, wind-graded equipment and technique frameworks, daily conditioning and wellness monitoring, and a briefing–debrief loop that turns each race day into the next day's plan — led by a PhD sport scientist and national-team coach.

The long game

This isn't a season. It's a ten-year runway to 2036.

India's target is the 2036 Olympic Games — and the athletes who could sail it are twelve to sixteen years old today. That is the whole logic of this squad: start now, build the base early, and give a generation ten uninterrupted years of international racing and professional coaching.

Techno 293 is where that pathway begins. Every race analysed, every athlete's development tracked year over year — so progress is measured, not hoped for.
