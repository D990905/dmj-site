Best speed by duration

knots · duration on a log scale
Both panels show the same seven numbers — the curve gives the shape, the table the exact values. The 2 s and 10 s points come from time windows; the rest are your best 100 m, 250 m, 500 m and 1 NM converted to how long they took. Duration runs on a log scale so a two-second burst and a two-minute run fit in one view. Alpha 500 sits off the curve because it is a gated course — 500 m out and back inside a 50 m gate — so it measures a speed you can turn around from, not a straight-line best.

Time at speed

minutes
Where your riding time actually went. The left-hand bars are time on the water below foiling speed — starts, stops, drifting and touchdowns — so a tall bar there is not a slow session, it is time not yet spent riding.
